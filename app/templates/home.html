Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the DealSignal property platform.

Is DealSignal free to use?

Yes, scanning postcodes and requesting direct cash offers is free. We are currently offering full investor toolsets free to access during our beta release phase.

How is the motivated seller data sourced?

We aggregate weekly public data feeds from live estate agency listings, tracking structural markers like price drops, length of time on the market, and historic transaction collapses.

What happens after I request a cash offer?

Our local partners evaluate your postcode context and property type immediately. A representative will contact you within 24 to 48 hours to discuss the direct valuation details.

How do I contact support?
